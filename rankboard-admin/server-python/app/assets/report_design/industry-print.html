Section 11

Strategy & Notes

Content strategy

Develop a content calendar and publish at least 6–8 blog posts per month targeting long-tail keywords and addressing common industry pain points.

Submission strategy

Submit the site to high-authority local business directories and social bookmarking sites to improve local SEO, backlink profile and referral traffic.

Off-page strategy

Keep NAP (Name, Address, Phone Number) details consistent across local directories, maps and citation sites to strengthen local SEO.

On-page strategy

Optimize every page for target long-tail keywords — keyword density, meta descriptions, title tags — and increase internal linking so deeper pages rank higher. Keep content fresh.

Competition strategy

Analyze top competitors’ backlink sources, content strategy and keyword rankings, and acquire backlinks from the same or higher-quality sites.

Keywords strategy

Target specific, less competitive long-tail keywords matching user intent, with a local focus on Perth, Sydney and Melbourne to drive geo-targeted traffic.
